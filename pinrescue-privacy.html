拔針救公主 隱私權政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 24 日

本政策說明行動遊戲「拔針救公主」（Pin Rescue: Save the Princess，以下稱「本遊戲」，開發者：Lin）如何處理資訊。

1. 本遊戲儲存的資料

本遊戲不要求註冊帳號。目前關卡、語言與音效設定只儲存在您的裝置上。開發者沒有伺服器、帳號或分析服務，不會收到這些資料。解除安裝本遊戲時，這些資料會一併刪除。

2. 廣告（Google AdMob）

本遊戲顯示 Google AdMob 提供的廣告。為了投放、限制與衡量廣告以及防止詐欺，Google 可能收集並處理以下資訊：

  • 裝置的廣告識別碼（Android Advertising ID）
  • IP 位址（用於推估大概位置）、裝置與應用程式資訊
  • 您與廣告的互動紀錄
  • 當機與效能診斷資訊

廣告是否個人化，取決於您的同意與所在地區的法規。這些資料由 Google 依其政策處理，詳見：

  • Google 如何使用合作夥伴網站或應用程式的資訊
  • Google 隱私權政策

3. 您的選擇

  • 位於需要徵求同意地區（例如歐洲經濟區與英國）的使用者，會在遊戲啟動時被詢問偏好，並可隨時在遊戲的「設定 > 隱私權選項」中變更。
  • 您可以在 Android 設定中重設或刪除廣告 ID。

4. 兒童

本遊戲並非以 13 歲以下兒童為對象，開發者不會在知情的情況下收集兒童的個人資料。

5. 資料安全

本遊戲與廣告服務之間的連線皆使用加密傳輸（HTTPS）。

6. 政策變更

本政策可能隨遊戲更新而修改，上方日期為最新版本。

7. 聯絡方式

如有任何問題，請透過 Google Play 商店「拔針救公主」頁面上的開發者聯絡方式與我們聯繫。

Pin Rescue: Save the Princess — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game "Pin Rescue: Save the Princess" (the "Game"), developed by Lin, handles information.

1. Information the Game stores

The Game does not require an account. Your current level, language, and sound setting are saved only on your device. The developer does not operate servers, accounts, or analytics and does not receive this information. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To deliver, limit, and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address (used to derive approximate location), device and app information
  • How you interact with ads
  • Crash and performance diagnostics

Whether ads are personalized depends on your consent and the laws of your region. Google processes this data under its own policies:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • Google Privacy Policy

3. Your choices

  • Users in regions that require consent (such as the EEA and the UK) are asked for their choices when the Game starts and can change them at any time from Settings > Privacy options in the Game.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

4. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Data security

Connections between the Game and the ad service are encrypted (HTTPS).

6. Changes

This policy may be updated when the Game changes. The date above shows the latest version.

7. Contact

For questions, contact the developer through the contact details on the Game's Google Play store page.
